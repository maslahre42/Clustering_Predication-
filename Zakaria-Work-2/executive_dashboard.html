Operations Research Engine & Mathematical Formulation

Under the hood of the Gurobi Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) core deployed for the City of Heilbronn.

1. Mathematical CVRP Formulation

Binary routing variables xijk ∈ {0,1} define vehicle k traversing edge (i, j) on the graph V = {0, 1, ..., N, N+1}. Miller-Tucker-Zemlin (MTZ) continuous variables eliminate subtours while strictly enforcing both volume (18.0 m³) and payload (9,000 kg) vehicle capacities.

2. Asymmetric OSRM Road Graph

Real urban street topography exhibits severe directional asymmetry (Dij ≠ Dji) due to one-way streets, roundabouts, and traffic turning rules. The engine computes full 111 × 111 travel distance and duration matrices using Heilbronn road graphs.

3. MTVRP Multi-Trip Shift Model

Refuse vehicles dump collection payloads at Entsorgungszentrum Böllinger Höfe (Node N+1) with a 20-minute turnaround (tunload), then immediately launch subsequent collection trips. All 4 runs finish in 6.45 hours, operating within a single 8-hour shift.

4. Independent Verification Auditor

Post-solve audit pipeline (verify_gurobi_routes) independently reconstructs every vehicle trajectory from raw GPS coordinates, verifying exact single-visit candidate coverage, road distances within 0.05 km, and zero capacity breaches.
